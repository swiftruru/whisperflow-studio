# v1.17.1

Fixes a long-standing bug in which the subtitle preview and editor showed every timestamp as `00:00:00` when they fell back to reading a `.vtt` file.

修正一個存在已久的問題：當字幕預覽與編輯器退回讀取 `.vtt` 檔時，所有時間碼都顯示為 `00:00:00`。

## Fixed / 修正

- **Reading a transcript back from a `.vtt` put every cue at zero** ([src/main/transcript-reader.js](src/main/transcript-reader.js)) — WebVTT makes the hours block optional, and our own writer omits it: `format_timestamp` adds `HH:` only once a cue is an hour in, or when `always_include_hours` is set, which only the SRT writer sets. So any recording whose speech ends before the hour mark produces a `.vtt` in which **every** cue is short-form (`04:09.018 --> 04:11.738`). `parseSrtTime` required the hours block and returned `0` when its pattern did not match, which is indistinguishable from a cue that genuinely starts at zero — so nothing failed. A real 715-cue transcript opened with every timestamp reading `00:00:00` and a total length of `00:00`, with the text itself perfectly intact and no error anywhere. The parser now accepts both forms, reads the fractional part positionally (`.5` is 500 ms, not 5 ms), and allows an hours field past 99 for very long recordings. A cue whose timing cannot be parsed is now **dropped rather than placed at zero**: keeping it put a cue at the start of the timeline that belonged somewhere else, and saving from the editor would have written that back over the file. This path is only reached when neither a `.json` nor an `.srt` sits beside the media — moving those two into a subfolder and leaving the `.vtt` behind is enough to trigger it. Present since at least v1.16.7.
  **從 `.vtt` 讀回逐字稿時每一則都落在零點** — WebVTT 規格允許省略小時欄位，而我們自己的 writer 正是這樣做：`format_timestamp` 只在字幕已跨過一小時、或 `always_include_hours` 被設定（只有 SRT writer 會設）時才加上 `HH:`。因此只要錄影的語音在一小時內結束，產生的 `.vtt` 裡**每一則**都是短格式（`04:09.018 --> 04:11.738`）。`parseSrtTime` 要求小時欄位必須存在，比對失敗時回傳 `0`——而那與「這則字幕真的從零開始」無法區分，所以不會有任何失敗訊號。一份真實的 715 則逐字稿就這樣以全部 `00:00:00`、總長 `00:00` 打開，文字本身完好無損，整條路徑沒有任何錯誤。現在解析器兩種格式都接受，小數部分依位數解讀（`.5` 是 500 毫秒而非 5 毫秒），並允許超過 99 的小時欄位以支援超長錄影。時間碼無法解析的字幕現在會被**丟棄而不是放到零點**：留著它會讓一則本該在別處的字幕跑到時間軸開頭，而從編輯器存檔時就會把那個結果寫回檔案。這條路徑只有在媒體檔旁邊既沒有 `.json` 也沒有 `.srt` 時才會走到——把這兩個搬進子資料夾、只留下 `.vtt`，就足以觸發。此問題至少自 v1.16.7 起即存在。

## Tests / 測試

- **Eight cases covering both timestamp forms** ([tests/unit/transcript-reader.test.js](tests/unit/transcript-reader.test.js)) — short form, long form with either fractional separator, a file that switches form at the hour mark (which is what our own writer produces for anything over an hour), positional milliseconds, a three-digit hours field, an unparseable timing being dropped, a cue that genuinely starts at zero being kept, and a whole short-form `.vtt` read the way the preview reads it. Five mutants killed: restoring the old pattern, restoring the `return 0`, keeping unparseable cues, reading milliseconds as a plain number, and capping the hours field at two digits.
  **八項測試涵蓋兩種時間碼格式** — 短格式、長格式（兩種小數分隔符）、在一小時處切換格式的檔案（那正是我們自己的 writer 對超過一小時的輸出）、依位數解讀的毫秒、三位數的小時欄位、無法解析的時間碼會被丟棄、真的從零開始的字幕會被保留，以及完整讀取一份短格式 `.vtt`（走預覽實際使用的路徑）。殺掉五個突變體：還原舊的比對樣式、還原 `return 0`、保留無法解析的字幕、把毫秒當成一般數字、把小時欄位限制為兩位數。
