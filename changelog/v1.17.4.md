# v1.17.4

Adds two translation-oriented profiles, and stops the console implying that Whisper's raw segments are the finished subtitles.

新增兩個翻譯取向的設定檔，並讓 console 不再讓人誤以為 Whisper 的原始段落就是最終字幕。

## Added / 新增

- **`EN-Translate` and `TW-Translate` profiles** ([python/config/EN-Translate/config.json](python/config/EN-Translate/config.json), [python/config/TW-Translate/config.json](python/config/TW-Translate/config.json), [.gitignore](.gitignore)) — for when the subtitles are going to an external translation service rather than onto a screen. Cue-by-cue translation is only as good as the cue: a cue ending mid-clause gets translated without the words that complete it. The default subtitle shape cannot avoid that, and it is arithmetic rather than tuning — on a real lecture, **36% of sentences are longer than 84 characters**, which is what two lines of 42 fit, so they have to be split somewhere. Raising the reward for cutting at a sentence end does not help: multiplying it by five moved the share of cues ending at a sentence from 58.8% to 60.5% while making four-word fragments *worse*, 3.8% to 6.2%. Widening the cue does help, because it removes the binding constraint. At 80 characters per line and a 12-second cap, measured on a 5-minute slice of a real lecture, cues ending at a sentence or comma go from 87.5% to **100%** and fragments of four words or fewer from 3.8% to **zero**. The trade-off is cues up to ~160 characters and 11 seconds — unreadable as film subtitles, fine as bilingual text being read. Both profiles also keep diarization and segmentation on. The Chinese width (30) is **derived rather than measured**: it is the same 1.9x relaxation that took Latin from 42 to 80, applied to the CJK norm of 16. There was no Chinese material long enough to measure on, and it is a single field to change if it proves wrong. Profiles are directories under `python/config/`, which `.gitignore` excludes so a user's own are never committed; these two are re-included by name, because they are part of the repo rather than something the user made.
  **`EN-Translate` 與 `TW-Translate` 設定檔** — 用於字幕要交給外部翻譯服務而非顯示在螢幕上的情況。逐則翻譯的品質上限就是那一則本身：結束在子句中間的字幕，會在缺少後半句的情況下被翻譯。預設的字幕形狀無法避免這件事，而且那是算術問題而非調參問題——在真實講座上，**36% 的句子長度超過 84 字元**，也就是兩行各 42 字元所能容納的量，所以它們一定得在某處被切開。提高「在句尾切」的獎勵沒有用：把它加強五倍，結束於句尾的比例只從 58.8% 升到 60.5%，而四字以內的碎片反而*變差*，從 3.8% 變成 6.2%。放寬字幕寬度才有用，因為那才是真正的綁定條件。每行 80 字元、上限 12 秒，在一段五分鐘的真實講座上量測：結束於句尾或逗號的比例從 87.5% 提高到 **100%**，四個字以內的碎片從 3.8% 降到 **零**。代價是每則最長約 160 字元 / 11 秒——當電影字幕不合格，當閱讀用的雙語文本剛好。兩個設定檔也都維持講者辨識與切段開啟。中文的行寬（30）是**推導而非量測**所得：那是把拉丁從 42 放寬到 80 的同一個 1.9 倍，套用到中日韓的規範值 16。手邊沒有夠長的中文素材可供量測，而它只是一個欄位，證明不對就改。設定檔是 `python/config/` 下的子目錄，`.gitignore` 會排除它們以免把使用者自己的設定檔提交上去；這兩個則以名稱重新納入，因為它們屬於這個 repo 而非使用者建立的。

## Fixed / 修正

- **The console made Whisper's raw segments look like the finished subtitles** ([python/whisperflow/transcriber.py](python/whisperflow/transcriber.py)) — `bridge/run_cli.py` forces `verbose` on, so the app's console scrolls every segment as Whisper decodes it, with chunk-relative timestamps that restart at `00:00:00` for each VAD chunk and lengths that re-segmentation has not cut down yet, because it runs after the whole Whisper pass. A reader has every reason to take those for the output and report a 27-second cue that no longer exists by the time the files are written. One line now follows the segmentation summary saying so, and carrying the real cue count and longest duration. Only when `verbose` is on: with nothing scrolled past, there is nothing to correct.
  **console 讓 Whisper 的原始段落看起來像是最終字幕** — `bridge/run_cli.py` 寫死開啟 `verbose`，所以 app 的 console 會在 Whisper 解碼時逐段滾動輸出，而那些時間碼是每個 VAD 分塊各自從 `00:00:00` 重新起算的，長度也還沒被重新切段處理過——因為切段是在整趟 Whisper 跑完之後才執行。讀的人完全有理由把那些當成輸出，然後回報一則 27 秒的字幕，而那則字幕在檔案寫出時早已不存在。現在切段統計之後會多一行說明這件事，並帶上實際的字幕則數與最長秒數。只在 `verbose` 開啟時出現：沒有東西滾過去，就沒有東西需要澄清。

## Tests / 測試

- **Eight cases** ([python/whisperflow/tests/test_config.py](python/whisperflow/tests/test_config.py), [python/whisperflow/tests/test_transcriber.py](python/whisperflow/tests/test_transcriber.py)) — both profiles exist and are tracked, each carries its intended language, width, duration and line count, and each is a *complete* config with the same key set as the template rather than a diff, so loading one does not depend on what the defaults happen to be that release. The console note is pinned to appear once with the real cue count, and to stay absent when `verbose` is off; a `_SegStub` runs the real `_run_segmentation` so the logging is actually exercised. Three mutants killed.
  **八項測試** — 兩個設定檔都存在且被版控追蹤、各自帶著預期的語言 / 行寬 / 時長 / 行數，而且每個都是**完整**的設定（鍵集合與範本相同）而非差異檔，所以載入它不會取決於該版本的預設值剛好是什麼。console 提示被釘住：會出現一次、帶著真實的字幕則數，而在 `verbose` 關閉時不出現；`_SegStub` 會執行真正的 `_run_segmentation`，確保那段 logging 真的被跑到。殺掉三個突變體。
